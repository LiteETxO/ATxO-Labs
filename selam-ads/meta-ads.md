## Notes for the media buyer
- **One campaign per angle** (names above) → clean ROAS read in HQ. Start all four, kill the losers in a week.
- **Placements:** 1:1 + 4:5 for feed, 9:16 for Stories/Reels. Instagram is included automatically (same campaign).
- **First 3 words carry it** on mobile feed — each short primary text front-loads the hook.
- Avoid overpromising specific numbers; keep claims to what the page states (autonomous, local, own-it, outreach).
