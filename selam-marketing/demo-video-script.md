## Production notes
- **Capture**: QuickTime screen recording of the app + a phone/webcam angle for shot 4 (you physically leaving frame sells it — pick the angle where both you and the screen are visible).
- **Audio**: her TTS is the co-star; duck music under her lines. No music during shot 4's pause — silence sells the pause.
- **Character**: use Mocha or Hazel for the hero session (distinct from every AI-demo-blonde cliché; also shows the cast range next to shot 2).
- **Cut-downs**: shots 4, 5, and 2 each work standalone as vertical clips for X — capture at full quality and crop later.
- **Honesty check**: record against the shipping 1.0.3 build, no dev flags. If a beat fails on camera, that's a bug report, not an editing problem.
