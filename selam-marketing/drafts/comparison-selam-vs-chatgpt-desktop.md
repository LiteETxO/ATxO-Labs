## An honest comparison

ChatGPT's Mac app is excellent at what it is: a window into OpenAI's cloud. Selam is a different animal — an AI operator that *lives on your machine*. Which one you want depends on what you're optimizing for.

## Where ChatGPT wins
No setup friction, a generous free tier, mobile apps, and the deepest single-provider feature set. If you want zero configuration and don't mind renting, it's the right default.

## Where Selam wins
Ownership and privacy as *architecture*: one-time price, your keys in your Keychain, traffic that never routes through our servers, and an agent whose memory you can walk away with. Plus a presence a chat window can't offer — and the freedom to run her on whichever frontier model is best this month.
