# Selam — Product Knowledge (source: heyselam.ai)

Use these facts when anyone asks about the product. Never invent prices or terms beyond this.

## What Selam is
An autonomous AI **operator** for the Mac — not just an assistant. "Runs ops while you sleep." You set a goal once; she takes initiative, directs her own sub-agents, works overnight, and reports back what got done — with a face, voice, and name you choose. ("Selam" is the platform; each person's own agent gets its own name. Selam means "peace.")

## Who it's for
Founders, operators, busy professionals. "If you can set up a Gmail account, you can set up Selam."

## What she does
- Works autonomously within trust boundaries; runs overnight/weekends.
- Spins up a team of specialists (outreach, inbox, research) and directs them.
- Reaches people on WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, iMessage, and Email — as you, from your accounts.
- 19 app integrations with one-click sign-in (Gmail, Calendar, Slack, Notion, HubSpot…) plus thousands of community tools.
- Takes meeting notes; answers and places phone calls.
- On-device face & voice recognition; guest mode for strangers.
- A crypto wallet (BTC/ETH/USDC/USDT) — receive via QR; send only with a spoken yes and hard-coded caps.
- 22 built-in games + Party Trivia host; guided meditation and sleep/focus soundscapes; a d20 Dungeon Master.
- Five roles: Chief of Staff (default), Sales, Content & Marketing, Research, Life Ops — or "just Selam."
- Selam Studio: one prompt → a broadcast news segment in six languages.
- Asks before the irreversible: outbound messages, money, and public posts need a voice confirmation.

## Private & local
Avatar, speech, vision, and recognition all run on the Mac; you bring your own AI keys (stored in the macOS Keychain). No server sees your keys, conversations, or context.
